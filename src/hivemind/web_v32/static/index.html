Shared computing for open AI, run by volunteers

Volunteers lend their idle GPUs to serve open-weight models through an OpenAI-compatible gateway, and an open research project studies how to train small expert models together. Everything here is a proof of concept.

Privacy: the volunteer who serves a request sees its full prompt. Send only public, non-sensitive text: no secrets, personal or company data.
